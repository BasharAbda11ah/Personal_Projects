# Meet The Cheese — Your Website

A website for **Meet The Cheese** that puts your full menu online for all three branches. Customers send their orders straight to the right branch on WhatsApp.

---

## What your customers get

**1. Every branch in one place**
On their first visit, customers choose **Ajman, Al Zubair or Kalba**. The site remembers their choice and shows that
branch's address, phone and WhatsApp number throughout. Customers can switch branch at any time.

**2. Your full menu, with your own photos**
All 138 items from your menus (burgers, zinger sandwiches, toast sandwiches, wraps, combo meals, chicken buckets,
appetizers, pasta, mojitos, shakes, smoothies, add-ons and sauces), with prices as printed and more than 100 photos from your menu artwork.
Customers can filter by category or search by name.

**3. A real cart and order summary**
Customers pick options (for example **Single** or **Meal**), add items, change quantities and see the total update straight away.
The cart is kept if they close the page and come back later.

**4. Orders arrive on WhatsApp**
When the customer is ready, one button opens WhatsApp with the whole order already written, sent to the branch they chose:

```
MEET THE CHEESE — ORDER REQUEST

Selected branch: Meet The Cheese — Ajman Branch
Branch location: Al Hamidiya 1, Ajman (Near Hyakum Car Service)

ITEMS
1. Classic Zinger — Meal
Quantity: 2
Unit price: AED 29.00
Line total: AED 58.00

2. Lotus Shake
Quantity: 1
Unit price: AED 14.00
Line total: AED 14.00

Subtotal: AED 72.00

Please confirm availability and the next steps for this order.
```

The customer presses Send, and your staff reply to confirm, exactly as they do with WhatsApp orders today.
The website never claims an order is confirmed. It tells customers clearly that the branch confirms by replying.

**5. Made for phones**
Most customers will use a phone. The site uses large buttons, a two-column menu, a slide-in cart, and a "Send order" button that stays at the bottom of the screen.

**6. English and العربية**
Customers can switch the whole site to Arabic with right-to-left layout. Product names stay exactly as on your menu.

**7. Ready for QR codes**
Each branch gets its own link (for example `…/?branch=kalba`) that opens the menu with that branch already selected,
ready to print as a QR code on tables, flyers or the shop window.

---

## What it does not do
- No online payments, no delivery checkout, and no customer accounts.
- The website does not send orders by itself. WhatsApp opens with the order already written; the customer presses Send,
  and your branch confirms or declines it by replying. Nothing is confirmed until your staff reply.
- Menu changes are made by your web developer (usually a few minutes per change).

## Possible future additions (not included, separate agreement)
These do **not** exist in the current website. Each would need your decisions and policies (prices, delivery areas,
fees, refunds), separate development, and usually a paid third-party service:
- Online card payments.
- Delivery ordering: addresses, delivery zones and delivery fees.
- An admin page so staff can edit the menu and prices themselves.
- Connecting orders to a POS, delivery apps or the WhatsApp Business API.
- Additional branches (for example Ras Al Khaimah).

## Privacy
The site asks customers for no personal details. It remembers only their branch, cart and language,
and only on their own phone. It uses no advertising or tracking tools.
