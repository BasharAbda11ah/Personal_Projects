## 1. Summary
A mobile-friendly website for Meet The Cheese's three branches (Ajman, Al Zubair, Kalba). Customers browse the full
menu in English or Arabic, build an order, and send it to their chosen branch on WhatsApp. A working preview can be shown before you decide.

## 2. Scope: included in the fixed price
1. **Website set-up and configuration** of the existing Meet The Cheese website build.
2. **Branch selection** for Ajman, Al Zubair and Kalba, with each branch's address and contact details.
3. **Digital menu** with the items, prices and product photos from your current printed menus, including category filters and search.
4. **Responsive design** for phones, tablets and computers.
5. **Cart and order summary**: options (e.g. Single/Meal), quantities, totals, and the cart saved on the customer's device.
6. **WhatsApp order requests** sent to the selected branch's WhatsApp number, with the full order prefilled.
7. **English and Arabic** interface.
8. **Branch-specific links** ready for QR codes (one per branch).
9. **Publishing** the website on a hosting account and domain owned by Meet The Cheese (see section 4).
10. **Handover**: a short guide explaining how the site works and how menu updates are made.
11. **Menu corrections** from your confirmation list (prices, option names, spellings) before launch.
12. **Revisions:** up to [2] rounds of minor changes (text, prices, photos, colours) requested within [14] days of the preview.

## 3. Not included (can be quoted separately)
Unless agreed separately in writing, the fixed price does **not** include:
- **Domain** registration and yearly renewal.
- **Hosting fees** or paid hosting plans (a free static-hosting plan is usually enough for this site).
- **Ongoing maintenance and support** after the revision period.
- **Menu and price updates after handover**: [AED ___ per change, or an optional monthly plan at AED ___ / month].
- **Additional branches** beyond Ajman, Al Zubair and Kalba.
- **Online payments** (card payments, payment gateways).
- **Delivery** checkout, delivery addresses, delivery zones or delivery-fee calculation.
- **Admin panel or database**, customer accounts, or an order-management system.
- **New features and future redesigns**, including integrations (POS, delivery apps, WhatsApp Business API).
- **Professional photography** or replacement images, logo or brand design.
- Printing QR codes or other marketing material.

How ordering works (so expectations are clear): the website opens WhatsApp with the customer's order already written.
The **customer sends the message themselves**, and the **restaurant confirms** (or declines) the order by replying.
The website does not take payments and does not receive or store orders.

## 4. Ownership and accounts
- The domain and hosting accounts are created in **Meet The Cheese's name** (or transferred to it), so the restaurant keeps full control.
- On final payment, Meet The Cheese receives the website files and the right to use them for its business.
- The restaurant confirms it has the right to use the logo, menu content and photos supplied for the website.
